The Urban Hypothesis

“The man who is traveling and does not yet know the city awaiting him along his route wonders what the palace will be like, the barracks, the mill, the theater, the bazaar.”

This – some say – confirms the hypothesis that each man bears in his mind a city made only of differences, a city without figures and without form.

“But why, then, does the city exist? What line separates the inside from the outside, the rumble of wheels from the howl of wolves?”

— Italo Calvino, Invisible Cities
